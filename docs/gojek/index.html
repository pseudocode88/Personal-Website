    • Designing a Spot Parking Application

    “Find a parking spot near places of major attractions like concerts, stadiums is a big pain in Jakarta; especially during special events and occasions when a lot of people are going to be there.”

    Currently there are about 1,000 parking areas, including about 420 off-street sites that are mostly privately run, and another 600 on-street parking spots, hardly enough to accommodate demand, particularly during peak hours. This document focus on designing a solution to handle the high-demand of parking across Indonesia.

    Parking Options

    Currently, in Surabaya, there are three parking options are available – On-Street, Off-Street and Building. On-street is where the parking slot is just in front of the shop or the building. Off-street is a couple of meters away from the street, like parking ground of a mall or an office. And Building is the multistory parking option. According to the supply of Surabaya, assuming that the parking option is same for Jakarta too.

    Trip Purpose

    Based on the report we can categorise the purpose of a trip into four - Shopping, Business, Work and Recreation. Among the four, the most popular is shopping, then followed by work and the least is recreation.

    So, why is it important to consider the trip purpose? Because the choice of parking location is dependent on the purpose of the trip, people who come for shopping choose building parking and people who come for work opt for surface parking.

    Parking Factors

    Along with the trip purpose, people also consider other seven factors to pick the best fit. They are Walking condition, Security, Fee, Convenient, Comfort, Closeness and Availability. The most exciting part is people tend to choose security over fees. The most considered factors are Security, Availability and Comfort.

    Attribute Mapping

    Briefing

    1. The majority of parking slot is consumed by people who come for shopping and working
    2. Parking location and trip purpose are dependent on each other
    3. People care about security more than fees
    4. Availability is the second most demanding factor to pick parking slot

    System Overview

    To break the ice, first designed a bird view of the parking system with main actors as Go-Jek, Jukir and Motorist.

    Go-Jek runs a web service containing real-time information about parking slots availability across Indonesia. To collect the real-time update, Go-Jek provides a mobile app for Jukir to validate and manage pre-bookings. And for Motorists, an app to access the parking slot updates, search through and book a slot.

    1st Iteration

    The primary goal of this iteration is to create an optimal flow for Mariam and Abdul to book a parking slot on the spot.

    User Flow

    Currently, on the home screen, the user needs to do six main actions to perform booking. All of them are inputs for the search criteria. The actions can be described as:

    1. Enter and search location
    2. Choosing the parking type
    3. Selecting the date & time
    4. Lookup and select parking locations
    5. Select vehicle type
    6. Book a slot

    Here the actions 1, 2 & 3 are required to find the availability, which is location, parking type, Date and Time. But we can move the option to select the vehicle on the next screen. And instead of the input, we can show the availability counter based on vehicle type. For, eg, like ‘car - 100 free slots’. This reduces an user-hop to goal which is always good. So on rewriting the user flow –

    Low-Fi Wireframes

    The primary focus of the step is the create a wireframe for the optimal path for a spot booking and stripping out all other complexities. Just focus on the flow.

    Home Screen

    • Primary action is the location search, and by default it takes the current location and selects the nearest parking slot.
    • Based on the case study, making the option to choose parking type as tab navigation instead of a drop-down filter. So the user can quickly switch between the options.
    • Date & Time as a button on top of the map and being a spot parking assuming that, it will be one of the less used filters. On tapping the button, it opens a popup with time picker.
    • Showing the parking lot and booking button as a card and show it just below the selected marker. On select, the marker the card get updated with the selected parking lot information.
    • Instead of popping up the parking location card, we can provide a slider, where the user can just swipe between the options. This interaction will help the user to avoid fiddling with the map and instead of that, easy to toggle between availability.
    • It's better to detach the booking button from the card because there is a chance it gets tap while the user slides the list.

    Payment Screen

    • In payment screen, the user can configure his parking by choosing the vehicle type and duration he needs to park.
    • Once the user gives the required input, show the price briefing
    • Simple Payment choice, pay by Go-Pay or Cash

    Ticket Screen

    • QR code to scan at jukir to validate the booking and start the parking timer.
    • Parking slot name and address
    • Parking slot date, time and duration
    • Button to open up the google map to get directions to the parking slot.

    Mockups

    Briefing

    Designed a four-screen solution to spot book a parking slot for both car and motorcycle. Currently, there's no option to book parking slot ahead. Also, there is an edge case where the user selects a last-minute slot, and on the payment screen when he configures the duration it might get overlapped, and he might not get the slot. In the next iteration, the focus will be handling the pre-booking and the edge case.

    2nd Iteration

    In this iteration, we are helping Ariya Hidayat to pre-book a parking spot for his next concert.

    Currently, the flow is perfect for spot booking; the user only needs to give the location and select parking type. But there are no screens for pre-book. Before designing, let's talk about the edge case mention in the last iteration's briefing. This case happens because of collecting the duration and vehicle type after selecting the parking slot. So duration is also a factor which matters availability of the slot. If the user sets the duration after choosing the parking place, there is a chance where he might not get availability.

    For example, consider the above chart and the following conditions:

    • Car parking - Today, 08:00am for 2 hours - Available
    • Car parking - Today, 08:00am for 1 hour - Available
    • Car parking - Today, 08:00am for > 2 hours - Not Available

    Screen & User Flow

    To handle the pre-book and the edge case, introducing two new screens to the home screen. One is the filter page for search, and another one is pass trunk, to keep and access passes.

    Low-Fi Wireframes

    Home Screen

    To accommodate pre-book, introducing a filter control just after the location input. On tapping the filter, it will open up the filter page with options.

    On tapping the passes button, the app will open the side drawer with quick access to todays and upcoming tickets. And it helps the user to access his passes quickly.

    Payment Screen

    Since we move the inputs from the Payment screen to the Home screen filter, now the main action of this page is to collect the payment for the book.

    Tracking Plan

    Defining a funnel to track the growth and success rate of the service, starting with booking request, followed by payment and scanning the pass. This funnel will help us to optimise the process and increase the success rate of booking by pointing out the funnel drop-outs.

    Keyword Why When Attributes
    Booking Requested Parking completion rate, Parking slot search success rate, funnel booking process Home screen, 'Book A Slot' button onTap location, parking_type, date, time, duration, vehicle_type and req_status
    Paid Parking completion rate, Payment success rate, funnel booking process Payment screen, 'Pay' button onTap payment_type, req_status
    Pass Cancelled Parking completion rate, funnel booking process Ticket screen, 'cancel' button onTap req_status
    Pass Scanned Parking completion rate, funnel booking process Ticket screen, onScanning at Jukir scanned_time, req_status
