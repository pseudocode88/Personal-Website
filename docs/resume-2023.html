Professional Experience

Head of Interaction Design - Web, Gojek

2018 to present

Gojek is a mobile-first company, but the core is still on the web. The command centre of all products is always internal tools and processes that are used on a daily basis. During the scaling era, the experience of such portals became a bottleneck because of the way each of them was designed and developed. This gives me the opportunity to prove the need for a dedicated web team with expertise in both design and development.

In 2018, I formed the official Gojek Web team with two designers, including me, and collaborated with UX Engineering to spin off the development entity. From day one, we focused on providing design and development services to internal teams and started working on our first product, the Asphalt Web Design System.

By the end of Q1 2019, we had released the first stable version of the Asphalt Web Design System and onboarded Cartography Tools as our first customer. Since then, we have onboarded more than 48 projects and saved an approximate US$1,474,560 in design and development costs.

By Q2 2019, I expanded the team scope to marketing by acquiring the web marketing team of 13, including front-end and back-end engineers, designers, and managers. And as the first initiative, we revamped the Gojek website to adhere to the new branding. As a part of this initiative, we released our second product, called Rambu, a marketing design system to bring brand consistency and improve production quality on all the marketing campaigns and microsites.

From 2019 to 2022, we focused more on providing design services to internal teams and collaborated with over 20 cross-functional teams to identify strategic revenue opportunities, streamline operations, boost productivity, elevate overall quality, and drive down operational costs.

By the beginning of 2021, the marketing campaign request had been doubled and had become a bottleneck in the resource allocation. If the request rate keeps increasing, eventually it will become an operational overhead for Gojek. This is where our third product, Prime Studio, comes into play. It's a marketing campaign automation tool that eliminates the need for designer-developer collaboration entirely. Upon release, we are able to reduce the campaign release time from two weeks to a single day, establish a centralised source of truth, and ensure consistent branding across all web marketing touch points.

In 2022, along with my responsibility, I took ownership of two cross-functional design teams (creative and data engineering) that were in need of strong leadership. And now, I'm in a transition phase to take responsibility for the entire interaction design team, including both the web and mobile.

Web3 Projects

TzCreator

Released on Tezos, tzcreator.art

Being an NFT art collector, after Ethereum, Tezos is one of the most preferred chains for NFT artists to mint and distribute artwork. But one of the missing pieces in the Tezos art ecosystem is artist sovereignty—the ability for the artist to easily create and deploy their own smart contract without any coding knowledge. To address this, we came up with the idea of introducing TzCreator, a free-to-use tool for the artist to create, deploy, and mint artwork using their own smart contract. Within a month of release and through word of mouth, 80 contracts have been deployed and 500+ artworks have been minted. This metric validated the product requirement, and we are in the process of expanding the feature to become an inevitable piece of the Tezos art ecosystem.


Publishing Studio

WIP on Ethereum

NFTs had opened up a new distribution channel for entertainment, which is still untapped by the majority. So we are taking this early opportunity to set up our own manga studio with in-house artists and writers. There are so many comics publishing platforms available, like Interpop, but they lack quality. That's the spot where we place us. Eventually, we should expand the studio into a publishing company, a seed funder, and a technology provider for all emerging artists. Right now, we are working on a 3-volume sci-fi fictional comic and multiple mini-comics. We aim to start the operation by the end of Q3 2023 and do the first release prior to the upcoming bitcoin halving.


Paper Greets

Multichain, Metaverse

Paper Greets is a concept to revive the long-lost culture of sending and receiving postcards in the metaverse as NFP, a.k.a. non-fungible postcards. Through this project, we envision that while the user navigates the meta-uninverse link sandbox and decentraland, there will be kiosks deployed on different properties where they can interact, select, buy, and send postcards to wallets. We are still in the ideation phase.
