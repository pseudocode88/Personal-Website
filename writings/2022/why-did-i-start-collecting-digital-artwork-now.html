    • Why did I start collecting digital artwork now?

    Jul 2022

    Why did I start collecting digital artwork now? What stopped me from collecting it before? How do NFT and other technologies encourage me to start?

    As a child, I collected a lot of things, from wrappers to tickets to matchboxes to any random good-looking thing that I found on the road. Over time, it became serious and narrowed my taste to postage stamps, postcards, vinyl records, CDs, and cassettes. Now NFT'ed artworks.

    Digital artwork was there even before the NFT, but what blocked me from collecting were mainly three factors.

    • Proof of Authenticity
    • Proof of Ownership
    • Preservation

    Proof of Authenticity

    Being a serious collector, I care about what I collect. Always do multiple checks before getting it, even from a whitelisted dealer. In a tangible world, it's quite hard to keep your collection authentic, and now imagine it in a digital world.

    Even if it's an NFT'ed artwork or not, the authenticity is still a question mark. In some premium marketplaces, there will be a dedicated team to verify and flag the fakes. But in high volume markets, it will be difficult.

    As a collector, there will be times when we face fakes and counterfeits, and it is an inevitable part of collecting. So it's always good to have a checklist, especially when purchasing from platforms that don't have a pest control system in place.

    Proof of Ownership

    This is where the NFTs play a role. Every NFT that is minted on a blockchain has a creator and an owner. And when the NFT gets transferred, it will get recorded on the blockchain. This transaction history is enough to track and prove the ownership.

    Preservation

    Even if you are an amateur or a professional collector, preservation is your prime responsibility. In the case of tangible items, you need to take care of multiple factors like dust, humidity, and pests, which will destroy your collection.

    For digital collectibles, you don't need to worry about dust or humidity. But, you need to worry about permanent data loss, which is more painful than the slow death of physical items. Imagine you saved all your digital artwork on a hard drive, and suddenly it stops working.

    Or you stored it in the cloud and the provider abruptly terminated the service. Both are equally destructive. So how can we preserve our collection forever?

    There are solutions like Arewave and Pinata available, but we are in a pre-mature stage, especially in preserving digital files. But by looking at the current initiatives and innovation happening in the storage space, the future is bright.

    To wrap-up, I would say we are at an early stage of collecting NFT'ed artworks. And we have good enough tools and solutions to take care of the collection. But the cognitive load of maintaining the collection is a bit high at the moment.

    Over time, I strongly believe this space will grow and become more accessible to even non-tech-savvy people by simplifying and automating the process of collecting, maintaining, and showcasing artwork.

    For me, I'm loving this new hobby and enjoying this space. And the best part is that I can talk with the artists/creators and get inspired; it's a different feel all together.

    Cheers to all the artists and collectors.
