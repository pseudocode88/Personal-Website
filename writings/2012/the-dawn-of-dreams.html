    • The Dawn of Dreams

    12 Jun 2012

    Drive my eyes through the nature on a silent night
    saw the star shining bright light over me
    saw moon light falling into the river, the river of peace
    saw the birds sleeping in the pines
    felt the wind, that touched me
    closed my eyes slowly and fell into
    the dawn of dreams.

    The sun is shining and spring everywhere
    i dont know where i am, but my mind bound high
    with full of joy and smiled
    i saw birds flying nearby
    i feel ther song
    i walked lonely
    the flowers near by
    touched me with joy
    i reached near a bank
    ther i lost my thirst
    it was a bank of an old river
    who flows calm and quietly
    near him i saw berries,
    blush and fleshy.
    i took them and walked.
    i reached a field where,
    the yield of peace is sowing
    far under a tree little ones sharing joy
    mild wind spread everywhere
    he make me cool
    i enjoyed the world with
    fulfilled peace and joy
    i heard a thunderbolt
    i fell from my dream
    and opened my eyes
    i saw cloud of smoke and fire
    i took my weapon and ran to the ground
    and just look back to my dream.
    there i saw a beautiful world,
    and i looked around,
    everyone is sowing hatred.
    it stumble my heart, my mind, my soul
    tear drops fall into my hand
    i throw my weapon into the ground
    and raise my head with strong heart,
    i said stop, peace.
    But fate played it well,
    i fell into the ground,
    blood gushes out from my heart,
    my eyes get filled with tears, and
    stars and the moon get blurred and blurred
    and blended to the tranquility of life.
