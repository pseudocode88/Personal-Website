    • Your Contract, Your Freedom

    Mar 2023

    A majority of artists started using their own contracts rather than marketplaces for minting and selling their work.

    What is causing the paradigm shift?

    Is it a fad?

    Almost all artists started their NFT journeys in marketplaces. But when we talk about artists' freedom, almost all marketplaces limit it by owning the minting contract.

    When you mint an NFT on a marketplace, you are actually using a shared contract owned by them. And they control the terms and administration of the contract. So, how much freedom can you buy by using your contract?

    The Freedom to Own

    You may have heard of the bitcoiner commandment: "Not your keys, not your coins." So what if I say, "Not your contract, not your tokens"? You may be the creator, but the minting authority owns it. Crypto is all about ownership.

    The Freedom to Control

    It's obvious that with ownership comes the power to control. You are the minting authority, and you are the one who controls the terms, like royalty and licensing, as well as the administration.

    The Freedom to Prove

    When talking about art, provenance plays an important role in defining the value based on the origin and ownership. And by owning your contract, it will make you and the contract the source of origin, and the transaction history tracks the ownership.

    The Freedom to Choose

    Right now, the operating model of the marketplace is a master-slave model. where the artists need to follow the terms and conditions put forward by them.

    By owning your contract, this will shift the model into a partnership model, where you and the marketplace agree on a mutual agreement that's beneficial for both. And you are free to choose with whom you want to partner.

    The Freedom of Being Borderless

    Geopolitical tensions have an impact on marketplaces. which you have seen recently where Iranian artists accounts are getting banned and removed from marketplaces. It could happen to you tomorrow.

    So, if you own your contract, you can sell it directly through the contract using a simple chain explorer. This makes you 100% borderless.

    The Freedom to Experiment

    By owning your contract, you are free to add interactive layers on top of your art. That means more opportunity to experiment and be creative. For example, burn to redeem, collect to unlock, token gated content, and even stake to airdrop.
