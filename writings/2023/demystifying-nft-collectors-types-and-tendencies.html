    • Demystifying NFT Collectors - Types and Tendencies

    Apr 2023

    Art collectors in the NFT space can be broadly categorised into four types, mirroring those found in the physical art world:

    • Investor
    • Flippers
    • Trophy Hunters
    • Enthusiasts

    Investor

    Collectors who aim to maximise their returns. They typically seek out 1/1 artworks and adopt a mid to long-term investment approach. Prior to making a purchase, they consider factors such as floor price, fame or notoriety, craftsmanship, and socio-cultural relevance. While they tend to favour established artists, they may also take calculated risks on emerging talent.

    Flippers

    Akin to day traders who aim to profit from short-term market fluctuations. They prioritise volume over high margins and typically sell artworks within a short window of time. Depending on the artist, some flippers may purchase multiple copies of an artwork, selling one shortly after the primary sale and holding onto others for later resale.

    Flippers can benefit the NFT market by creating liquidity, increasing visibility, and establishing market prices. This can benefit artists by increasing demand for their work and providing an easy way for collectors to sell their NFTs.

    Trophy Hunters

    Trophy hunters are collectors who are primarily interested in owning rare or highly sought-after NFTs as a way of demonstrating their wealth or status. They may be less interested in the artistic value, and more interested in its rarity or exclusivity.

    Enthusiasts

    Collectors those who are primarily interested in the artistic or cultural value of artwork. They may be less focused on the financial potential of the NFT, and more interested in owning a piece of digital art that they find meaningful or aesthetically pleasing.

    By collecting and appreciating NFT art, enthusiasts help to support and promote artists, especially emerging artists who may not yet have a large following. This can help to cultivate a thriving NFT art community.

    Enthusiast collectors tend to focus on building a collection that reflects their personal taste and interests, rather than trying to acquire the most valuable or popular artworks. This can lead to a collection that is more meaningful and fulfilling for the collector.

    However, as NFT art becomes more accessible, we are witnessing a new type of collector emerge, one that defies categorisation by combining elements of all four. We can call them Hybrid collectors.

    Hybrid collectors may be interested in NFTs as a form of investment, but they also appreciate the artistic and creative aspects of the medium. They may collect both established and emerging artists, and they may hold onto some NFTs for the long term while flipping others for a quick profit.

    Hybrid collectors tend to have a more diverse portfolio of NFTs, and they may be more flexible in their approach to collecting than collectors who strictly adhere to one type.

    Up until now, the classifications have been focused on individual collectors. However, it's important to note the existence of another category of NFT collectors - Institutional collectors.

    Institutional collectors are organisations or institutions that collect NFTs, such as museums, galleries, universities, and other cultural institutions. They may acquire NFTs depending on their mission and area of focus. For eg: a museum may collect NFTs that are relevant to its collections or exhibitions, while a university may collect NFTs that are relevant to its research or academic programs.

    In terms of how they collect, institutional collectors may acquire NFTs through a variety of methods, such as purchasing them on NFT marketplaces, working directly with artists or collectors, or collaborating with other institutions.

    In conclusion, the NFT space has brought forth a diverse range of art collectors, each with their own unique approach to collecting.

    From investors to enthusiasts, flippers to trophy hunters, and hybrids in between, NFTs have created a dynamic and rapidly evolving market. As the NFT space continues to grow and evolve, we can expect to see even more innovative and diverse types of collectors emerge.
