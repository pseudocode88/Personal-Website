    • Is AI Just a Fancy Search Bar?

    Aug 2024 • AI

    I get this question all the time from friends, colleagues, and even my mentees: Can I really use AI for more than just finding stuff?

    Honestly, you're the expert on your life. But let me throw in my two cents.

    To get the most out of AI, you've gotta figure out where it fits into your world. We all have routines, right? Whether you're crushing it at work, trying to learn something new, or just trying to navigate life, you probably have a system.

    I know what you're thinking – diagrams and fancy flowcharts. Nope, not necessary. A simple to-do list or a step-by-step plan will do the trick.

    So, find those spots in your routine where you think AI could be a game-changer. But don't just slap it in there and expect magic. Try it out, see what happens. Does it save you time? Make things better? Or is it just plain fun?

    The key is to experiment without stressing about perfection. Be a pioneer, not a follower. There's something really cool about being one of the first people to figure out how to use this stuff. So go ahead, have fun, and see where it takes you.
