    • Building Design Infrastructure: Your Brand's AI Future Starts Now

    Mar 2025 • AI

    Design systems are no longer optional. They're evolving into design infrastructure, and too many organizations are still treating them like a nice-to-have.

    I've watched this play out more times than I'd like. Execs check the box. Designers jump in with energy. Then things quietly fall apart. No broader vision, chaotic scaling, shifting priorities. That shiny new design system becomes a half-finished relic nobody maintains. It's not a resource problem. It's a clarity problem.

    Here's why this matters more than ever right now.

    We're entering an era where a significant chunk of UI design will be generated by AI. Not someday. Soon. But AI doesn't conjure good design out of thin air. It learns from what you feed it. Consistent tokens, solid components, clear documentation. If your design system is incomplete or inconsistent, that's exactly what your AI-generated output will reflect. Garbage in, garbage out, at scale.

    Get the foundation right, and AI becomes a genuine multiplier. Skip it, and you'll spend more time cleaning up broken, off-brand output than you would have spent building properly from the start.

    The good news: you don't need a large team or a big budget to begin. AI tools are already here to help you move faster. LLMs can scaffold cross-framework code, draft documentation, and prototype components while you're still in planning mode. The "we're too lean for this" excuse doesn't hold up anymore.

    A design system isn't a project you finish. It's something you tend to. Start small. Iterate. Treat it like the strategic asset it is, because that's exactly what it will be when AI becomes a core part of your design workflow.

    That future isn't far off. The organizations investing now will be ready for it. The ones who wait will be catching up.
