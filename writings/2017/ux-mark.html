    • Fitts's Law, Skeuomorphism and Cockpit

    01 Nov 2017

    Cockpit is a Tmux workspace manager built by my amigo, Rabi C Shah. First, he made this tool to solve his own problem of managing terminal workspaces, but later, he thought of releasing it to other fellow developers in the community. But before the release, he wants to improve the aesthetics as well as the usability of the tool. So he asked me to help him to re-design Cockpit.

    New Project, New Workspace!

    Rabi is someone who, spends a reasonable amount of time in learning and hacking new things. Even while I'm writing this article, his sitting next to me and learning Elm. To manage all his projects, his use Tmux, and writes a bash file for each project, and load it using a program called Shuttle. But later he got fed up with writing workspace scripts again and again. So he thought of automating it. This spark turned out into a three-day project, and he made an HTML5 app that can generate a workspace bash script with a couple of trackpad clicks.

    Small or big, cockpit is a smart work and smart people always automate!
